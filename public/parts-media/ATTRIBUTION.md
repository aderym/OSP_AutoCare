# OSP AutoCare original component reference artwork

Creator: OSP AutoCare. Original programmatic schematic drawings, created for this
library. No third-party photographs or OEM drawings are included in this selection.

The SVG artwork in assets/parts-media and its PNG renders in public/parts-media
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

This dedication applies only to this library's artwork, not the app code,
trademarks, existing external image results or other repository assets.

Each manifest record identifies the creator, license, source SVG, component ID
and SHA-256 of the rendered PNG. Captions retain creator and license information.

These images are generic illustrations and annotated identification schematics.
They are not OEM drawings, not to scale and do not verify VIN compatibility.
They are not service procedures, wiring pinouts or installation instructions.
