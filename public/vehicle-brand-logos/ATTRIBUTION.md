# Vehicle brand identification assets

Brand logos remain trademarks and property of their respective owners. Used only to identify vehicle makes, without endorsement or affiliation.

Sources: filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset (Carlogos.org image collection), vehiclespecs/brand-logos, and avto-dev/vehicle-logotypes. Each manifest entry records the source URL, collection and SHA-256 of the bundled asset. No remote image loads are required at runtime.

Collection structure and metadata are MIT licensed where provided; that license does not grant rights in third-party trademarks. See collection-license.txt for the vehiclespecs notice. No emblem is invented for manufacturers without a verified source; those entries use the application's generic vehicle icon.

Supplemental exact article-linked logos were retrieved from Wikimedia/Wikipedia. Their file pages, authors and license labels are recorded individually in manifest.json.
